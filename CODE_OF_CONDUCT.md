# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Spoki-App/claude-limits-window-shift/security/advisories/new)
marked as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.
